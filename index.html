Privacy Policy — FF Sensi One

Last updated: 4 October 2026

Android · com.ffsensi.ff_sensi_all_in_one

This policy explains what FF Sensi One (“the app”, “we”) does with information when you use it. The app is a sensitivity companion tool for Free Fire players.

The short version. The app has no accounts and no sign-in, and we do not run any server. Your sensitivity setups stay on your phone. The app does use Google AdMob to show ads and Google Analytics for Firebase to measure usage, and those services collect data under their own policies.

Information you enter

Everything you create in the app is stored locally on your device and is never uploaded to us:

  • Saved sensitivity setups and their names
  • Your current sensitivity profile
  • Sensitivity history in the Tracker, including any notes you add
  • Your device profile answers (platform, performance, FPS, DPI, gyroscope)
  • Starred presets and your most recent analysis score

We cannot see this data. It is removed when you use Settings → Clear Saved Data or uninstall the app.

Advertising

The app shows ads supplied by Google AdMob, in interstitial, rewarded and app-open formats. Google may collect and use data to serve and measure those ads, including your device’s advertising identifier, approximate location derived from IP address, and ad-interaction data.

  • Google’s handling of this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, and opt out of ad personalisation, in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the app is used so we can improve it. It records screen views and a small number of events: opening a tool, saving a setup, running an analysis, applying a preset and adding a Tracker entry.

These events record that an action happened. They do not include your sensitivity values, setup names, notes, or any other content you type into the app. Firebase also collects standard technical information such as device model, operating system version, country and a randomly generated app instance identifier. See Firebase’s privacy information.

Permissions

The app requests internet access, which is required to load ads and send analytics. It also uses the advertising ID permission (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID) for the reasons above. It does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, files, precise location or any other sensitive permission.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help resolve it.

Keeping and deleting data

Because we hold no server-side copy of your content, deleting it is entirely in your hands: clear it in the app’s settings, or uninstall the app. To limit what Google receives, use the Android advertising controls linked above.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date at the top.
